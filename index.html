Module 07 // Portfolio

Work samples & case studies

Projects below are clearly labelled samples. Real client work will be added here as it is approved for publishing.

Illustrative case study

A retail shop moves from paper billing to simple software

Problem
Daily sales were recorded in a paper register. Stock counts were unreliable and monthly totals took hours to calculate by hand.
Solution
A simple billing and inventory tool with a product list, quick bill generation, daily sales summary and a low-stock alert, plus a small website with a WhatsApp order button.
Outcome
Billing takes seconds instead of minutes, stock is visible at a glance and monthly totals are available without manual counting.
Illustrative case study

A coaching centre gets more admission enquiries

Problem
Parents could not find course details, fees or batch timings online. All enquiries came through word of mouth.
Solution
An admission-focused website with course pages, faculty details, a short enquiry form, WhatsApp click-to-chat and a properly set up Google Business Profile.
Outcome
Enquiries arrive through the website form and WhatsApp instead of only by phone, and staff can follow up from one list.
