MODULE 02 // DEEP DIVE

Service details

Website Development in Patna

This is for shop owners, clinics, coaching centres and small firms who need a website that answers basic customer questions and collects enquiries. We plan the page structure with you, write clear headings, build a mobile-first layout, connect a contact form and WhatsApp button, and set up basic SEO titles and descriptions. Typical outcome: a fast site you can share on Google and WhatsApp, with enquiries arriving in one place instead of scattered across calls.

Up to 5-10 pages, mobile responsive Contact form + WhatsApp button Basic SEO and speed setup

Custom Software Development in Patna

Best suited to businesses that have outgrown registers, spreadsheets or three different apps. We start by writing down the exact workflow: who creates a record, who approves it, what report you need at the end of the month. Then we build a focused tool — billing, inventory, bookings or customer records — with roles, backups and a simple screen your staff can learn in a day. Typical outcome: less duplicate data entry and a clear view of daily numbers.

Workflow mapped before coding Roles, reports and data backup Short milestones with progress updates

Mobile App Development in Patna

For businesses whose customers are on phones all day: retail shops, clinics, coaching centres and service startups. We decide together whether a simple Android app or a cross-platform build is the right spend, then design a small number of screens that do one job well — browse, book, order or check status. You get a tested build on real devices, a clear update path and a support window after launch. Typical outcome: repeat customers who open your app instead of searching for your number.

Android or cross-platform build Tested on real devices before launch Play Store listing help

Data Analytics in Patna

Useful when you already collect data but never look at it. We connect your website, WhatsApp enquiries, billing sheet or sales register into one simple dashboard showing the numbers you check weekly: enquiries, conversion, best-selling items, repeat customers. You get a short walkthrough so you can read the dashboard yourself, plus a monthly summary format. Typical outcome: decisions about stock, staff and marketing based on numbers instead of guesses.

One dashboard for key numbers Analytics and conversion tracking Monthly summary you can read

SEO Services in Patna

For businesses that already have a website but get no calls from it. We start with a technical check — page speed, mobile layout, broken links, duplicate titles — then fix page titles, descriptions, headings and internal links. We set up or correct your Google Business Profile so you appear in local map results, and give you a simple content plan for the pages that actually bring enquiries. Typical outcome: slow, steady improvement in the searches that matter. No ranking guarantees are ever made.

Technical audit and fixes Titles, descriptions and headings Google Business Profile setup

Digital Marketing in Patna

Built for owners who want enquiries this month, not vanity numbers. We agree one clear goal, pick two channels you can maintain (usually Google Business Profile plus one social platform), and prepare simple creatives and captions in Hindi or English. Every campaign links to a page that can actually convert, and we report enquiries and cost per enquiry rather than likes. Typical outcome: a repeatable monthly routine with numbers you can check yourself.

Goal and channel plan first Creative in Hindi and English Enquiry-focused reporting

Cloud Setup in Patna

For teams that lose time on hosting renewals, expired domains and broken email. We document what you currently own, move sites to a reliable host, connect the domain and SSL correctly, set up business email and configure automatic backups. Everything is written into a one-page handover so you are never locked in or dependent on memory. Typical outcome: fewer outages, faster page loads and a clear list of yearly costs you can budget for.

Domain, hosting and SSL configured Business email and backups Written handover document

IT Support in Patna

For businesses that already have a website or software and simply want someone reliable to call. Support covers small content updates, bug fixes, plugin and security updates, backup checks and basic device or email help. We agree a response window and a monthly hour allowance in writing, so you know what is covered and what is charged separately. Typical outcome: problems fixed before they cost you customers, with one predictable monthly line item.

Agreed response window Updates, fixes and backup checks Monthly hour allowance
