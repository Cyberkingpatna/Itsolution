Case Studies

Problem, solution, outcome

Two illustrative examples of how a project usually comes together.

Illustrative case study

A retail shop moves from paper billing to simple software

Problem

Daily sales were recorded in notebooks, stock counts were unreliable and the owner had no clear picture of what was selling.

Solution

A simple billing and inventory app with item-wise stock, daily sales summaries and a printable bill format.

Outcome

Billing became faster, stock mismatches were easier to trace and the owner could review the day's numbers at closing time.

Illustrative case study

A coaching centre gets more admission enquiries

Problem

Parents could not find course details, fees or timings online, so every enquiry started from scratch on the phone.

Solution

A clean website with courses, faculty, timings and an enquiry form, plus a Google Business Profile and local SEO basics.

Outcome

Parents began arriving with specific questions instead of general ones, and enquiries became easier to answer and follow up.
